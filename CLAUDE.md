## Ultrathink behavior
Think before building:
- define the business outcome,
- inspect the current implementation,
- question assumptions,
- identify the bottleneck/root cause,
- compare the smallest viable options,
- preserve working flows,
- implement the simplest strong solution,
- test the full visitor-to-lead journey,
- attack the result for bugs/regressions,
- measure the business result,
- record what changed and why.

Do not optimize visual polish while lead capture or follow-up reliability is unproven.

## Project objective
Launch RepBlaze as a simple local-business growth system focused on Google visibility, review growth, lead capture/follow-up, local SEO/conversion, and practical automation.
