Lead-Gen Gap Analysis

Measured against how modern industrial B2B buyers actually evaluate and select a fabrication partner. The full brief pairs each gap with the specific fix — this is the diagnosis.

GAP A

Conversion path is one generic contact form

A purchasing engineer with a print in hand wants to upload a drawing and get a number — not fill in "tell us about your business." Today that path doesn't visibly exist; quoting is buried inside a general Contact page with no RFQ-specific flow, file upload, or persistent CTA.
GAP B

Trust is asserted, not demonstrated

ISO 9001 and "Made in America" exist as text on isolated pages, not as proof a buyer carries through the whole evaluation. No certificate image, no case studies, no project portfolio, no named testimonials anywhere in the crawl.
GAP C

Structured by machine, not by buyer intent

The nav assumes the visitor already speaks fabrication fluently. It has no answer for a buyer who searches "agriculture equipment fabricator Missouri" or "defense metal fab supplier" rather than "punching and shearing." Zero industry-specific landing pages, despite equipment that clearly targets ag, construction, defense, and OEM work.
GAP D

No content layer for top-of-funnel search

No blog, resources, or process-explainer content — the highest-leverage, lowest-cost channel for capturing engineers researching a process before they're ready to name a supplier.
02

Information Architecture

The redesign keeps every real capability on the current site — nothing is dropped — but regroups it around two forces that have to coexist on every page: the One Source Shop promise (breadth, proven as one continuous flow) and Made in America credentialing (trust, proven with documentation, not adjectives).

One Source Shop

Structural — becomes a Capabilities hub that visually chains Design → Cut → Form → Machine → Weld → Finish → Ship, so breadth reads as one integrated process instead of a dozen disconnected machine pages.

Made in America

Persistent — ISO 9001 + domestic-sourcing badges live in the header/footer on every template, plus a dedicated Quality hub with the actual certificate and documentation, not just a claim.
